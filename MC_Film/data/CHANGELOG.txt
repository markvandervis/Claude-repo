mcfilm_tables.json is generated by tools/build_dctl.py --profiles <dir>:
  * spectra resampled from 5 nm to 10 nm (380-780 nm, [1/4 1/2 1/4] kernel)
  * dye and base densities: unmeasured bands at the spectrum ends filled with
    the nearest measured value (layer sensitivities: unmeasured = 0)
  * characteristic curves resampled to 64 uniform samples and made
    non-decreasing
  * per negative: 3x3 exposure matrix (Mallett & Yuksel 2019 sRGB basis x
    the stock's reference illuminant x layer sensitivities, normalised to the
    green layer's 18 % grey exposure) and 18 % grey log exposures
  * per negative x print: print balance offsets (18 % grey prints neutral at
    density 1.0 under the viewing light) and the balanced 18 % grey print
    exposure
  * print spectra pre-multiplied with the printer lamp (our 3200 K model) and
    with the viewing light x CIE 1931 observer
